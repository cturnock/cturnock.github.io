About

Hi I'm Cody! I'm a product designer who enjoys untangling messy problems.

Over the last decade I've worked across complex B2B products, most recently designing software for commercial fleet managers at General Motors. The problems I enjoy most rarely start with pixels – they start with unclear requirements, competing priorities, technical constraints, and getting cross-functional teams aligned around the right problem before designing the solution.

I'm at my best when a project feels ambiguous. I enjoy asking the questions that change the direction of a project, challenging assumptions that no longer hold up, and working with Product, Engineering, Legal, and leadership to find solutions that are practical, scalable, and grounded in how people actually work.

The work I'm most proud of isn't necessarily the flashiest. It's the work that still makes sense months after launch because it solved the right problem, not just the one that was originally written down.

Outside of work, I'm based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, where life is mostly centered around my two kids. When I'm not designing, you'll usually find me tinkering in my garage, playing games, rolling dice for a D&D campaign, or tackling another project around the house.
